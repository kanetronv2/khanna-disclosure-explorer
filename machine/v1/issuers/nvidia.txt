## Did the reported range increase from 2024 to 2025?

The aggregate reported range's bounds both increased, from $851K – $1.77M in 2024 to $1.21M – $2.55M in 2025. The filing years are not directly comparable: The filing’s notes page states that, pursuant to the 2026 Instruction Guide, “the value bracket that represents the percentage interest in the asset is selected.” On that basis the 2025 brackets describe a percentage interest rather than the value of the asset itself, so 2025 totals are not directly comparable with earlier filing years and the year-over-year change should not be read as a change in wealth.

Calculation: **$851K – $1.77M** in 2024 to **$1.21M – $2.55M** in 2025.
