Frequently asked questions

Does Ro Khanna trade stocks?
The disclosures list 50,744 financial transactions from 2017 to 2026, including 44,080 classified as common stock. They cover Khanna, his spouse and dependent children, so a listed trade does not show who directed it.
How many financial transactions are in the disclosures?
50,744 across 2017–2026, of which 44,080 are classified as common stock. The latest year (2026) has 4,849, in reported value bands totalling $21.6M – $118.5M.
Whose trades appear in Ro Khanna's disclosures?
The forms label interests by owner code. Across loaded filings, spouse account for 68.1% of rows. Those labels do not establish beneficial ownership or who placed an order.
Has Ro Khanna supported a congressional stock trading ban?
Yes. A December 2023 Khanna proposal would bar members of Congress from holding or trading individual stocks. This site presents that position with the filings; it does not allege wrongdoing.
Where does this trade data come from?
We transcribe official House Clerk scans, cross-check them with OCR, and link each row to its source. Amounts are the form's value bands, not exact trade values.

Sources and method

We transcribe House Clerk filings, link each record to its source page, and flag uncertain readings.

  • Totals add the disclosed value bands. Where a band has no ceiling, the displayed upper sum is a floor.
  • Owner codes identify whose interest was reported. The filings do not identify who placed a trade.
