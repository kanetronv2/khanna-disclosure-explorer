# Ro Khanna reported stock trades, 2017–2026

> Canonical page: https://www.rokhanna.money/stock-trades/
> Dataset version: 2026-08-18

The loaded House filings contain 48,920 reported household transactions. Amounts are statutory value bands, not exact trade values, and the filings do not establish who made an investment decision.
